# Zenith-Mind — AI Ethics and Responsible AI Governance

**Algorithmic Fairness | Employee Privacy | AI Risk Assessment | Human Oversight**

**Master of Artificial Intelligence — Seneca Polytechnic**

## Project Overview

Zenith-Mind is a fictional AI-based employee retention risk assessment system developed by Zenith Logistics. The system uses workplace activity and personal data to predict employee disengagement and potential turnover.

This individual portfolio analysis evaluates the ethical risks associated with employee monitoring, inaccurate predictions, proxy discrimination, privacy violations, and automated management decisions.

## Technical Audit

The fictional case study evaluates 100 employee records.

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| True Positives | 40 |
| False Positives | 15 |
| False Negatives | 10 |
| True Negatives | 35 |
| Recall | 80% |
| Precision | 72.7% |
| Accuracy | 75% |

The findings show why predictive performance must be evaluated alongside the consequences of false classifications.

## Key Ethical Challenges

- **Proxy discrimination:** Employees leaving work for childcare responsibilities may be incorrectly classified as disengaged.
- **Feedback loops:** Management actions based on high-risk scores may contribute to the employee dissatisfaction the system predicts.
- **Contextual integrity:** Vending-machine purchase records may be reused for employee monitoring without appropriate justification.
- **Sensitive inference:** Combining wearable heart-rate data with purchase records creates privacy risks.
- **Accountability:** Managers, developers, HR leadership, and executives share responsibility for preventing harmful decisions.

## Proposed Governance Recommendations

1. Validate predictive features and investigate potential proxy discrimination.
2. Evaluate false-positive and false-negative consequences before using risk scores.
3. Restrict secondary uses of employee data and minimize sensitive information.
4. Require meaningful human review before employment-related actions.
5. Establish clear RACI responsibilities and independent ethics oversight.
6. Monitor incidents, employee complaints, and unintended feedback loops.

These are proposed governance measures, not implemented or experimentally validated interventions.
